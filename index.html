About Lumière

We Transform Spaces Into Living Art

Founded in 2012, Lumière Interiors is a boutique design studio dedicated to creating spaces of extraordinary beauty and function. Our team of award-winning designers brings a curated eye, deep craft knowledge, and genuine passion to every project — from intimate apartments to landmark commercial spaces.

We believe that a truly great interior doesn't just look beautiful — it feels like home the moment you walk in. Every material, every light source, every piece of furniture is chosen with intention.
